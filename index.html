Pandu: Learn Chinese Characters 🐼

Every Chinese character has a story. Learn how the pieces fit together.

Learning Chinese characters doesn't have to mean memorising thousands of unfamiliar shapes. Many characters are built from components that appear again and again. Recognising them can help you understand what characters mean, how they're pronounced, and how they're formed.

Pandu helps you discover these connections by starting with the building blocks of Chinese writing: radicals.

🌱 START WITH RADICALS

Radicals are an important part of the Chinese writing system. Learn to recognise them, understand their meanings, and see how they appear in Chinese characters. As you become familiar with these recurring components, you'll begin to recognise patterns across the language.

🧩 UNDERSTAND HOW CHARACTERS WORK

Why do some Chinese characters contain the same component? What can a character's parts tell you about its meaning or pronunciation?

Pandu explores the components that make up Chinese characters, like semantic components that provide clues to meaning and phonetic components that can offer clues to pronunciation. Instead of seeing a character as one complicated shape, learn to recognise the smaller parts that give it structure.

🔗 CONNECT RADICALS TO CHARACTERS

Learning a radical is only the beginning. Pandu helps you connect what you've learned to the characters that use it. Each connection gives you another opportunity to recognise familiar components and understand how Chinese characters relate to one another.

The more patterns you recognise, the less unfamiliar new characters can seem.

✍️ LEARN TO WRITE

Understanding a character's components is one part of learning it. Practise writing Chinese characters and become familiar with their strokes and structure as you study.

📚 BUILD YOUR MANDARIN KNOWLEDGE

Work through character lessons and HSK-related content as you develop your understanding of written Chinese. Whether you're starting from scratch or strengthening your existing knowledge, Pandu helps you build on what you've already learned.

🏆 SEE YOUR PROGRESS

Keep learning, complete lessons, and earn achievements as you make progress. Pandu's heart system adds a little challenge to your practice and encourages you to pay attention along the way.

A DIFFERENT WAY TO LEARN CHINESE

Chinese characters can look intimidating when you first encounter them. But once you start recognising their components, recurring patterns begin to emerge.

Pandu puts those connections at the heart of learning. Rather than focusing only on remembering individual characters, it helps you understand the building blocks behind them, giving you a foundation you can carry into your future Mandarin studies.

Whether you're learning for the HSK, studying Chinese at school, or preparing to use Mandarin in everyday life, understanding characters is an important step towards reading Chinese with greater confidence.
