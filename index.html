Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Pandu is a Mandarin-learning app that helps people learn Chinese radicals and characters through structured lessons, recognition exercises, writing practice, and progress tracking. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you use Pandu and describes rights that may apply under data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who We Are

Pandu is operated by Pandu, based in the Netherlands.

For privacy questions or requests, contact ..

For the purposes of the GDPR, the operator identified above is the data controller for personal data processed through Pandu, except where another organisation acts as an independent controller.

2. Information We Collect

The information processed depends on the features available in the version of Pandu you use and how those features are implemented.

Learning progress

Pandu may process learning-related information such as characters learned, radicals mastered, exercise results, achievements, and daily streaks. This information is used to provide progress tracking and maintain your learning experience.

Account information and Google Sign-In

Pandu may allow you to sign in using Google Sign-In. If you choose this option, Pandu receives information made available through the sign-in configuration and the permissions you grant. This may include your name, email address, Google account identifier, and profile picture. The actual information received depends on the implementation.

We use account information to authenticate you, identify your Pandu account, and provide account-related features. Pandu does not receive your Google password. Google processes information under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, relevant purchase records and subscription status may be processed to verify your entitlement and provide paid features. Purchases are handled by the applicable app store or payment provider. Pandu does not collect or store full payment card details unless it explicitly provides a payment service requiring them.

If a subscription-management service such as RevenueCat is integrated, it may process purchase receipts, app-specific identifiers, subscription status, and related transaction information.

Technical and diagnostic information

Depending on the services integrated into the released app, Pandu or its providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app. This may include device or operating-system information, app version, crash reports, and diagnostic data.

3. How We Use Information

We may use personal information where necessary to provide lessons and track learning progress, maintain achievements and streaks, authenticate accounts, manage subscriptions, keep the app secure and reliable, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Advertising

Pandu is designed without banner advertisements. If rewarded advertisements are introduced, for example an option to watch an advertisement to earn additional hearts, the advertising provider may process information to deliver the advertisement, measure its performance, prevent fraud, or determine advertising eligibility.

The information processed will depend on the provider and its configuration. Where required, we will provide appropriate notices and obtain consent before enabling technologies that require it. Relevant providers will be identified in this policy when they are integrated into the released app.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Pandu may offer an optional paid subscription for premium learning features. Purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play are subject to the applicable store's payment and privacy terms. The store processes payment information and may provide transaction information needed to validate a purchase.

If a subscription-management service is used, it may process information such as purchase receipts, app-specific user identifiers, and subscription status to manage access to paid features. Subscription cancellations and refund requests are handled through the applicable app store or payment provider, as appropriate.

6. Storage and Data Processing

Learning progress and other app information may be stored on your device or on external servers, depending on how each feature is implemented. If online storage or synchronisation is provided, information associated with your Pandu account may be stored on external servers to support account functionality and may be linked to your account identifier.

Google and other third-party services process information according to their respective service configurations and privacy policies. We will not claim that information remains exclusively on your device if external storage or processing is used.

7. Third-Party Services and Sharing

Depending on the features included in the released app, Pandu may use service providers for Google Sign-In, app distribution, in-app purchases, subscription management, advertising, hosting, storage, or diagnostics.

We do not sell or rent personal information. Providers acting on our behalf may process information as needed to provide their services, subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements. Some providers, including Google and app stores, may also process information under their own privacy policies.

We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8. International Transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where personal information is transferred outside the EEA, we will use an applicable legal transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, where required by data protection law.

You may contact us to request further information about relevant safeguards.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. Learning progress is retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant features. Account information may be retained while an account is active and afterward where necessary for operational or legal purposes.

Purchase records may be retained for periods required by tax, financial, or consumer-protection laws. Technical and diagnostic information is retained only for as long as necessary for its intended purpose. Actual retention periods depend on the systems and providers used by the released app.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, or a portable copy of certain information. You may also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions. To make a request, contact us at .. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

If Pandu provides account creation, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal information. Some information may need to be retained where required or permitted by law.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In the Netherlands, the supervisory authority is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

11. Children's Privacy

Pandu is intended for people learning Mandarin. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where the app is available to children, we will apply the consent requirements and other protections required by the laws applicable to those users.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances that violate applicable law, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Pandu's features, data-processing practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before making changes that require it.
